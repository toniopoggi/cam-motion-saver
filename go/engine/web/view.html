About Cam Motion Saver

Version 0.5.14-preview

Turn your Android phone into a local recorder for compatible Google Nest cameras.

Cam Motion Saver uses your phone to receive the camera stream, record motion events and store MP4 videos locally. A five-second pre-motion buffer helps capture what happened before an event.

Browse recordings with animated previews, play and download clips through a browser on your local network, and share a separate viewing link that keeps recorder controls private.

Choose how many days to keep your recordings, with automatic cleanup when they expire.

Your phone is the recorder, the server and the storage.
